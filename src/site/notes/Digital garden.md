A digital garden is ==a personal online collection of interconnected notes, evolving ideas, and raw thoughts==. Unlike traditional blogs or social media feeds, it features non-linear organization and evergreen, works-in-progress content. 
Key Characteristics

- **Non-linear Structure:** Notes connect via hyperlinks and context rather than chronological order or strict categories.

- **Evergreen Evolution:** Content grows over time through frequent edits, expansions, and pruning instead of remaining static like a published article.

- **Low-friction Publishing:** Focuses on raw learning in public rather than polished final drafts, reducing perfectionist pressure. 

Core Differences From Blogs

- **Blogs:** Chronological streams, polished final outputs, isolated single topics.

- **Digital Gardens:** Context-driven maps, living works-in-progress, highly interconnected thoughts.
