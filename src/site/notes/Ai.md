Artificial intelligence (AI) is ==computer technology that enables machines to simulate human learning, reasoning, and problem-solving==. It processes large sets of data to recognize patterns and make decisions autonomously. 
How AI Works

- **Data Collection:** Gathers large amounts of text, images, or numbers.

- **Pattern Recognition:** Uses algorithms to analyze information and find trends.

- **Prediction and Action:** Makes decisions or generates new content based on trained models. 

Core Subsets of AI

- **Machine Learning:** Systems improve performance automatically through experience and data without being explicitly programmed.

- **Deep Learning:** Uses layered artificial neural networks to mimic human brain function for complex tasks like speech and image recognition.

- **Generative AI:** Focuses on creating new outputs like text, images, or code from simple user prompts.
